About US

Our journey is built on 18 years of experience in business, with a strong understanding of entrepreneurship, operations, sourcing, customer requirements and business development across different sectors.

We bring this experience into the food and baking industry and specializes in B2B manufacturing and supply of baked products, chocolates, and confectionery, serving bakeries, cafés, retailers, food businesses, and small outlets.

But our vision goes beyond manufacturing and supplying products.

Bake to Business Program

Unlike traditional baking academies, we don't just teach baking help aspiring bakers build, launch, and grow their own businesses by providing the knowledge, resources, infrastructure, and guidance required to start and grow.

Baking Practice Center

A professional space where bakers can practise their skills, improve techniques, test recipes, and develop quality products using commercial equipment and facilities helping them build confidence and prepare for business.

Baking Incubation Center

A dedicated facility that enables bakers to use professional equipment and commercial kitchen space to develop, produce, and sell their products helping them start and grow their baking business without the need for a full kitchen investment.

We believe baking can be more than a skill it can become a profession a business and profitable and sustainable businesses.
